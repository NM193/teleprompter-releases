Teleprompter 1.1.0

Screen recording: record the entire screen, one window or part of the screen, with your microphone, a draggable camera bubble, and a recording bar with pause, start over and discard. A new Recordings window to play, share and manage recordings (⌃⌥R to record). The overlay under the notch can now follow your mouse to another monitor (Settings → Overlay). Screen recording needs macOS 15 or later.
