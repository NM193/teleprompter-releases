Teleprompter 1.2.0

Notes mode: show a script as cue cards with tabs, questions, answers and reminders (great for interviews), switch with ⌃⌥1–6 and ⌃⌥↓/↑. A video editor: join recordings, trim, split, change speed, and give the video a background, padding, rounded corners and a 16:9, 9:16 or square shape. Zoom on clicks: hold ⌥ and click while recording to zoom in there. See-through overlay (⌃⌥T): faint and click-through. The overlay can follow your mouse to another monitor.
